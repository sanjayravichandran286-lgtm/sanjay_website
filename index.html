Research & Exploration

CURRENT RESEARCH INTEREST

SpectrumSense

Making the Invisible Signal World Visible.

SpectrumSense explores the possibility of building a unified platform capable of observing, organising and analysing signal information from multiple physical domains.

The research focuses on adaptive data management, relationships between different signal observations, unknown signal intelligence and historical signal reconstruction.

The current phase focuses on understanding the fundamentals, evaluating feasibility and studying suitable technologies before implementation.
